# 1.Problem Title
Reducing Middlemen in Agricultural Marketing to Ensure Fair Prices for Farmers and Affordable Prices for Consumers.
# 2. Problem Creator's Organization
Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
# 3. Theme
Agriculture and Rural Development – Digital Agriculture and Supply Chain Management
# 4. Problem Description
Farmers often depend on multiple intermediaries (middlemen) to sell their agricultural products. Each intermediary adds extra charges and profit margins, reducing the income received by farmers and increasing the prices paid by consumers. This creates an unfair pricing system and affects both farmers and customers. Lack of direct market access, proper storage and transportation facilities makes the problem worse.
# 5. Proposed Solution
Develop a Farmer-to-Consumer Digital Marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers
 # 6.GPS Integration: Connect nearby farmers with consumers based on location.
Payment Gateway: Enable secure digital payments.
Database Management: Store farmer details, product information and order records.
Real-Time Price Updates: Display current market prices to help farmers make better selling decisions.
# 7. Feasibility and Viability
Technical Feasibility: The platform can be developed using existing technologies such as React.js, Node.js, MongoDB and GPS services.
Economic Feasibility: Development and maintenance costs can be managed through government support and agricultural organizations.
Operational Feasibility: Farmers and consumers can use the platform through smartphones.
Scalability: The platform can initially serve a small village and later expand to multiple districts and states
# 8. Impact and Benefits
Higher Farmer Income: Farmers can receive a better share of the selling price.
Affordable Prices: Consumers can purchase products at reasonable prices.
Reduced Middlemen: Direct connections reduce unnecessary intermediaries.
Transparency: Farmers and consumers can view product prices clearly.
Fresh Products: Direct distribution can reduce delays in reaching consumers.
Digital Empowerment: Encourages farmers to adopt modern technology.
Economic Growth: Supports rural development and improves the agricultural marketing system.
The Farmer-to-Consumer Digital Marketplace aims to create a transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain. By connecting farmers directly with consumers, this solution can improve farmers' earnings, reduce consumer expenses and promote the growth of digital agriculture.
